## 🔬 Current Research

### MFMD-TLI Soil Moisture Sensor

**Adaptive Multi-Frequency Multi-Depth Soil-Moisture Sensor with Tunable-Length Interchangeable Electrodes**

My research focuses on:

- Multi-frequency soil moisture sensing
- 1–30 MHz RF characterization
- 10 cm, 15 cm, and 20 cm interchangeable electrodes
- AD9850 DDS
- AD8307 RF detector
- ESP32
- Gravimetric soil-moisture calibration
- Adaptive frequency selection
- Soil-specific calibration
- Laboratory and field validation
